3.1 Segmentation

Both families were trained on the same frozen night split (manifest 170c442d…), with the same ten channels, focal-Tversky loss, patch sampling and 100-epoch schedule. The designed difference is the decoder. Seeds 42–46 change only the initialisation and the random crops. The test set holds 161 positive and 147 negative scans and shares no night with training or validation. Every run had already selected a probability threshold of 0.15 on validation; that cutoff was not refit.

Macro Dice on the positive test scans is 0.526 ± 0.005 for the Attention U-Net and 0.517 ± 0.009 for the U-Net (mean ± standard deviation over five seeds). Pixel-pooled Dice on the same scans is 0.654 ± 0.006 and 0.651 ± 0.003. The gap is about one macro-Dice point. It is smaller than the range among the five U-Net seeds, and much smaller than the drop from validation to test.

3.2 Paired comparison

The contrast named before the test set was opened is seed 42 against seed 42, on the same 161 positive scans. Nights are the resampling unit: 2,000 draws, 21 nights, percentile interval. Attention U-Net minus U-Net is +0.0037 Dice, with interval −0.0084 to +0.0170. The interval includes zero. IoU, precision and recall likewise include zero. Normalised surface distance does not: +0.0206 (0.0082 to 0.0319). Fuzzy boundary F1 does not either: +0.0179 (0.0062 to 0.0290). On the overlap metrics, the pre-specified seed does not establish a difference. On the boundary metrics, it does.

3.3 Robustness

The same paired contrast was repeated at each matched seed, without retuning the threshold. Dice remains positive at all five seeds. Its interval excludes zero at seeds 44, 45 and 46, and includes zero at seeds 42 and 43. The boundary advantage does not survive the repetition. At seed 43 the NSD difference is −0.0079, and the interval (−0.0133 to −0.0020) excludes zero in the U-Net’s favour. At seed 46 the NSD interval includes zero.

Figure 4 asks whether the boundary result depends on the 2 px tolerance at which NSD is reported. For the pre-specified seed the Attention U-Net curve lies above the U-Net curve from 1 px through 20 px. The night-clustered bands overlap, so the separation at a single tolerance should not be read as a gap that is resolved at every distance.

A perfect presence gate, which blanks every quiet scan and leaves positive scans untouched, cannot change macro Dice. The measured change is 0.0000 for both seed-42 models (0.5175 and 0.5138). Global Dice, which counts false pixels on quiet scans, rises by 0.0514 for the Attention U-Net (0.6047 to 0.6562) and by 0.0512 for the U-Net (0.5955 to 0.6467). The overlap score on positive scans is invariant to knowing which scans are quiet. The integrated score is not.

Validation macro Dice lies between 0.553 and 0.571. Test macro Dice lies between 0.504 and 0.532. The mean drop is 0.041 for the Attention U-Net and 0.042 for the U-Net. The architecture gap is small beside that shift, and the shift has the same size in both families.

3.4 Presence

Scan presence is read from the segmented area at the locked pixel threshold. A night score is either the maximum or the mean of those areas inside the night. Figure 5 shows the test ROC and the precision–recall curve for seed 42. Scan ROC area is 0.83 and 0.84. Night-maximum area gives 0.81 and 0.82. Night-mean area gives 0.86 and 0.86. Average precision follows the same order. The two decoders are not separable on these curves. The horizontal line on the precision–recall panel is the prevalence of positive scans in this cohort; negatives were subsampled, so that average precision is not an operational rate. The protocol also calls for the same curves on the Swin-Tiny scan probability. Those curves are absent: the seed-42 classifier weights are not on disk and not on the checkpoint mirror.

3.5 What is not estimated

The five Swin-Tiny seeds have validation ROC areas 0.941, 0.948, 0.927, 0.919 and 0.932. They were not scored on the test set. The cascade was not run. Development runs on the earlier, unbalanced split are omitted, because their nights are not these nights. The historical dashboard model is omitted for the same reason: it was trained where most nights appeared in more than one split. A lower Dice than that model’s published figure is the expected consequence of removing the leakage.
